About Accessible Place Finder

Accessible Place Finder is an inclusive civic platform dedicated to breaking down physical barriers in urban environments. We empower individuals with mobility challenges, wheelchair users, caregivers, and families with accessible strollers to explore cities with complete confidence.

Accessibility Standards We Follow

  • ADA Compliance: Verifying slope grades (minimum 1:12 for ramps), door clearance widths of at least 32-36 inches, and designated accessible parking stalls.
  • Digital Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA & AAA): High-contrast color modes, readable scalable typography, screen-reader semantic landmarks, and full keyboard navigation.
  • Community Verification: Every user-contributed review and report helps maintain accurate, timely ground truth.

How to Contribute

Anyone can browse places without an account. When you register a free account, you can submit star ratings, write detailed facility reviews, and flag inaccurate information. Administrators inspect and verify community reports to ensure information remains accurate.
